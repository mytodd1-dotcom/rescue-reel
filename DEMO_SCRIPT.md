# Rescue Reel — Three-Minute Demo

## 0:00–0:20 — The problem

“A rescue team can have a real emergency, willing supporters, and no time to
turn scattered updates into trustworthy media. Rescue Reel turns one urgent
note into one approved, verifiable campaign.”

Show the hero and the live provenance badge.

## 0:20–0:55 — Preserve and understand

Scroll to Maple’s intake. Point out that the note stays unchanged.

Click **Extract a truthful campaign**.

“Rescue Reel separates the confirmed facts—the Friday deadline, the injury,
the transport blocker, and the pledged funds—from the persuasive campaign
language.”

## 0:55–1:30 — Generate visibly

Click **Build the generated rescue reel**.

“The media step is observable. The approved generated image, grounded
narration, and vertical motion treatment remain connected to the source
instead of becoming an anonymous file.”

Show the animated Maple frame and the pipeline event stream.

## 1:30–2:10 — Human approval

Click **Approve & verify the B2 archive**.

“Nothing is treated as publishable until a person approves the exact draft.
That approval is the boundary between generation and action.”

Open **Proof trail**.

## 2:10–2:45 — Real B2 and Genblaze proof

“This is not placeholder text. The run ID, provider and model, B2 object path,
asset SHA-256, and canonical manifest hash come from a real archive written and
read back through Genblaze.”

Point to the live B2 receipt and verified event stream.

## 2:45–3:00 — Why it matters

“Rescue Reel gives small teams the speed of generated media without asking
them to surrender truth, approval, or accountability. The story can be
emotional. The evidence cannot be imaginary.”
