Rescue teams do not need another content generator. They need a faster way to
turn a real emergency into action without losing the truth along the way.

This is Rescue Reel.

It starts with one messy note about Maple: a foster deadline, a recovering leg,
a transport problem between Wichita and Kansas City, two hundred dollars
already pledged, and one clear contact deadline.

The original note stays visible and unchanged. When the coordinator asks Rescue
Reel to extract a campaign, the system separates confirmed facts from
persuasive language. It keeps the Friday deadline. It keeps the medical detail.
It keeps the pledged support. Then it turns those facts into one useful request:
offer Maple a fourteen-day foster home.

Next, Rescue Reel builds the media package. The campaign image, grounded
narration, and vertical motion treatment stay attached to the same source
instead of becoming anonymous files. The interface shows the generation step
as an event, not a mystery spinner.

But generated does not mean approved.

A person must approve the exact campaign before Rescue Reel treats it as ready
for action. That decision is the boundary between fast automation and careless
automation. If the draft changes, the approval must change with it.

Now open the proof trail.

Every check is visible: source preserved, claims grounded, generated media
attached, human approval recorded, and the Backblaze B2 archive verified.

This receipt is real. The run ID, provider and model, B2 bucket, object path,
asset hash, and canonical manifest hash came from an approved asset archived
through Genblaze and read back from Backblaze B2. The public interface exposes
the evidence without exposing credentials or temporary signed links.

Genblaze is the provenance and orchestration layer. Backblaze B2 is the durable
home for the approved media and its canonical manifest. Together, they let a
small rescue team answer a simple but important question: what exactly did we
approve, and can we prove it later?

The workflow is also budget-aware. If a paid provider is unavailable, Rescue
Reel stops the request. It does not hide the failure, invent a completed result,
or trigger surprise spending. An already approved asset can still move through
the verified archive path.

That is the product: need, truth, media, approval, action, and memory.

Rescue Reel gives small teams the speed of generated media without asking them
to surrender accountability. The story can be emotional. The evidence cannot
be imaginary.
